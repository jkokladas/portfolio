Background

My career has moved from the classroom to instructional coaching to academy-wide leadership. Along the way I’ve stayed focused on the same question: how can we enhance student voice and opportunity by helping faculty grow and feel empowered?

Today I bring that question to AI literacy, curricular alignment, and the way we welcome and grow faculty.

  1. Director of Professional Development & Instructional Design

    Shady Side Academy, Pittsburgh, PA

    Leading AI literacy and integration, curricular alignment, faculty onboarding and mentoring, and professional learning across four PK–12 campuses.

  2. Innovative Teaching & Learning Lead

    Shady Side Academy

    Led PK–12 STEM and digital literacy work aligned to ISTE and CSTA standards, and the academy’s distance learning playbooks.

  3. Technology Integration Specialist

    Shady Side Academy

    Partnered with teachers to bring technology, STEAM, and design thinking into classroom instruction.

  4. Instructional Technology Facilitator

    Davidson County Schools, North Carolina

    Built STEM programming and maker spaces aligned to CSTA and NGSS standards, and co-taught coding lessons across grade levels.
